# WARDEN: world bible (living)

## Pitch
A widescreen third-person action RPG. You create the Warden and name them. Cyber-medieval: Game of Thrones grit (oaths, gaols, wardens of the march) under neon, rain and humming pylons.

## Tone
Dark, gritty, sparse neon. Palette: near-black greys, the Warden's red/magenta synth glow, teal for the Rot and for city lights. Fog is constant and heavy at street level.

## The Warden
Matte black plate, tattered black mantle, smooth helm with a red T-visor and cables into the backplate. Unapologetic and ruthless, but fair and professional: they uphold the law of the land, not their own mercy. Every story choice should offer harsh-but-lawful options, never "nice" ones that break the code.

## The Synthblade
A longsword with one synth edge that cuts almost anything. Red/magenta edge glow, crackling arcs, red mist. Its glow grows with rarity and Cores.

## Hallowmere
A walled city. The rain has not stopped in eleven years. The Wall is a ring of power pylons. The Ninth Ward sits against the Grave Gate. The Tribunal is the colossal spire to the north where the law is written.

Districts so far: Lantern Street, Statute Plaza (statue of the first Warden, the Forge), Cutpurse Alley, the Grave Gate court.

## The Rot and its abominations
Beyond the Gate, the Rot fuses people with scrap and machinery. Teal bioluminescence marks it.
- Hollowed Husk: hunched, long-armed, plated with scrap. Basic melee.
- Skitter: six-legged crawler with a glowing sac. Fast, lunges.
- Bile Spitter: bloated, keeps distance, lobs teal bile.
- The Bellwright (elite): a bell-ringer that became the bell. Swipes, charges, and rings shockwaves.

## Systems vocabulary
- Rarity: Issued (grey), Forged (green), Tempered (blue), Relic (violet), Sovereign (gold).
- Cores: chips slotted into gear (Don's call: not "enchantments"). Each raises the item one tier. Arc, Ember, Leech, Bastion, Kinetic, Overclock, Edge. Graded Mk I to III.
- Tempering: upgrade +1 to +10 at the Forge with scrap.
- Skill branches: Blade (the edge of the law), Bulwark (the wall that does not fall), Circuit (the current in the steel).
- Verdict traits from judgments: Dread, Measure, Inquest.

## Characters
- Gaoler Brann: Ward gaoler, lantern, tired. His son sold a pylon key (Inquest path).
- Mira Toll: scavenger who stole a pylon cell for her brother's lung-pump.
