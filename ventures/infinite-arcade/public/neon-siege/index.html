About Neon Siege

An arena survivor in neon. Your ship fires on its own, so your job is to move, collect shards, and pick upgrades each level: split shots, piercing rounds, orbit blades and shockwaves. The swarm grows every second and an elite arrives each minute.

Controls

WASD or arrow keys, or drag anywhere to steer. Esc to pause. 1 to 3 to pick upgrades.

Works on phones, tablets and computers. Your best score is saved on this device.
