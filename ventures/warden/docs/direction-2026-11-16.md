Arc, Ember, Leech, Bastion, Kinetic, Overclock, Edge stay as provisional categories. FOR NOW: remove rarity-promotion; preserve existing Core functionality where practical; NO new Core rules, slots, progression, or crafting without approval.

Preference: player has some innate attunement, not exceptional; NOT a chosen one/divinely selected. Synthblade awakened = contained synth barrier along inset edge channel; dormant blade still a functional sword. Precise Awakening conditions discovered via gameplay/training, no elaborate tutorial. Do NOT establish religious blessing, bloodline, or named ritual as universally necessary.
