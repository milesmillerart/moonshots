About Duality

A fresh logic puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Fill the grid with suns and moons so every row and column has three of each and no three match in a line. Keep your streak alive, share your time, and play unlimited practice puzzles.

Controls

Tap a cell to cycle it, or use the arrow keys and Space. 1 for sun, 2 for moon, Z to undo.

Works on phones, tablets and computers. Your best score is saved on this device.
